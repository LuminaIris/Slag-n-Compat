
Slag n' Compat
=======
This addon to Slag n' Embers adds support for materials from many other mods.



Mod Compatibility
=======
Materials
-------
| **Mod**                 | **Materials**                                                   | **Traits**                                              | **Status**  |
|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|-------------|
| Allthemodium            | Allthemodium, Vibranium, Unobtainium                            | Unbreakable                                             | In Progress |
| Eternal Starlight       | Deepsilver, Starlit Diamond, Aethersent, Golem Steel, Unrealium |                                                         | In Progress |
| Ice and Fire: CE        | Fire Dragonsteel, Ice Dragonsteel, Lightning Dragonsteel        | Dragonslayer, Fire Imbued, Ice Imbued, Lightning Imbued | In Progress |
| Iron's Spellbooks       | Arcane, Mithril, Pyrium                                         |                                                         | In Progress |
| Mekanism                | Refined Obsidian, Refined Glowstone                             |                                                         | In Progress |
| Twilight Forest         | Ironwood, Steeleaf, Knightmetal, Fiery                          | Igniting, Auto-smelting                                 | In Progress |

Traits
-------
General
- Igniting: Lights enemies hit on fire
- Unbreakable: Tool does not take any durability damage

Ice and Fire
- Dragonslayer: Attacks deal extra damage to dragons
- Fire Imbued: Attacks deal extra knockback and ignite enemies; same as IaF fire dragonsteel tools
- Ice Imbued: Attacks freeze enemies; same as IaF ice dragonsteel tools
- Lightning Imbued: Attacks cause chain lightning to damage other enemies near the target; same as IaF lightning dragonsteel tools

Iron's Spellbooks
- Arcane: Attacks deal bonus magic damage based on your maximum mana
- Havocking: Attacks cause an explosion
